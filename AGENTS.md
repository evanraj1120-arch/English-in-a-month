# Project Instructions

These instructions apply to the entire project. The original class packet, `H-Eng 10_ MIDTERM.pdf`, is the primary source for educational content. Keep that source file intact.

## Educational accuracy

- Treat provided class materials as the primary source of truth for exam expectations, class terminology, practice, and student work.
- Never fabricate textual evidence, quotations, book-page references, teacher instructions, assignment requirements, or answer keys.
- Label teacher requirements, teacher examples, teacher notes, student-generated interpretations, and external research separately. Do not imply that a student answer is teacher-approved.
- Preserve the student's original arguments and interpretations unless explicitly asked to revise them. If a claim seems questionable, retain it and add a review note.
- Do not introduce outside literary interpretations as if they came from class. Label any future external research clearly and keep it separate from source-supported class content.
- When book page numbers or OCR are uncertain, preserve what the packet says and mark the uncertainty. Do not silently normalize quotations, spelling, or citations.

## Content quality

- Prefer specific, concrete explanations that help a student perform an exam task.
- Explain what a literary element does in its particular passage or novel; do not stop at a generic definition.
- Connect evidence to claims with explicit reasoning.
- Avoid repetitive explanations and filler while preserving meaningful nuance.
- Keep related concepts distinct (for example, tone and mood, symbol and motif, or irony and sarcasm).
- Use direct language suited to timed exam responses.

## Development quality

- Keep educational records in `public/content/` and their types/loading logic in `src/content/`; do not embed study content in UI components.
- Reuse components and consistent typed data structures. Keep the app responsive and accessible.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies, backend services, and infrastructure.
- Keep source inventory, verified study content, practice questions, and user progress as distinct data concerns.
- Never overwrite or delete the original PDF or existing content during source updates. Extend or version records and retain provenance.
- Verify the build and affected interactions after meaningful changes. Do not claim a feature works unless it has been checked.

## Project continuity

- Incorporate future uploads into the existing source/content model; do not rebuild completed sections without a reason.
- Track processed source ranges and the classification of their contents in `SOURCE_INVENTORY.md`.
- Record uncertainties, corrections, and decisions in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` and `DECISIONS.md`.
- Review prior records that could be affected when new material or teacher corrections arrive.
- The four-novel structure is fixed for v1: *1984*, *The Catcher in the Rye*, *The Metamorphosis*, and *The Glass Castle*. Catcher content is expected to expand later.
